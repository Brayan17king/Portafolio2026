This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you visit this portfolio site, operated by Brayan Martínez ("I", "me", "this site").

What this site does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-ups.
  • No cookies and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • No data is sent to or stored on any server operated by me. This is a static site.

What is stored locally on your device

This site uses your browser's localStorage only to remember two preferences between visits:

  • Your selected language (English/Spanish).

This information stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Contact form / email

The "Get in touch" action opens your own email client via a mailto: link. Nothing is submitted to or processed by this site — the message goes directly from your email client to my inbox, the same way any email would.

Children's privacy
